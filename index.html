2025 — 2026 · Time series

Hybrid Multi-Horizon Cryptocurrency Forecasting

A multiscale ADA/USDT forecasting pipeline using Python, pandas, NumPy, LightGBM, PyTorch, and statistical models. Features include lagged, rolling, volatility, return, and technical-indicator signals across multiple time resolutions.

The hybrid approach combines ARIMA, gradient boosting, convolutional autoencoders, ResNet-based representations, and horizon-specific fusion models, with leakage-safe temporal alignment and strict train/validation/test procedures.
